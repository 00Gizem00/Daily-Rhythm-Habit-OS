# Daily Rhythm beta — tester guide

Organizer: send this only after the candidate and distribution are approved. Provide the install invitation separately. Support and private beta feedback: **Contact@lumetechllc.com**. No invitation or email has been sent by this preparation task.

1. Install the approved build through its TestFlight invitation. If you already use Daily Rhythm, save **Habits → Data & Privacy → Export JSON**, keep the app installed, and update it in place. Check your plans/history afterward. Keep the backup somewhere you control.
2. Start with one small habit or one-off task. Choose a full target and, if helpful, a smaller light target. Record a completion and check the saved result. Try Undo only on a disposable step you just created.
3. Use it naturally for two weeks. A light day or a skipped day is useful feedback, not a failure. Widgets and ordinary Shortcuts are optional; tell us which one you tried.
4. Notifications start off. Enable them only if you want reminders. iOS controls permission and delivery; manual tracking works without them.
5. Diagnostics are optional and start off. If you want setup timing included, enable **Optional local pilot diagnostics** on the first setup screen. Later controls are in **Habits → Data & Privacy → Observe a 30-day pilot**. Turning it off deletes local observation; tracking remains available.
6. After days 7 and 14 have ended, you may choose **Export diagnostics JSON**, inspect the file and share it privately with the organizer. This is different from **Export JSON**, which contains your actual habit data. Never send a routine backup for ordinary pilot feedback. Reports are not sent automatically.
7. For a problem, open **Habits → Help & Beta** and include the version/build, iOS version, action and surface, expected outcome and actual outcome. TestFlight also offers **Send Beta Feedback**. Remove private text from screenshots first. Do not delete the app or use Erase Local Data to troubleshoot missing records.

The beta has no account, cloud sync, AI routine generation or purchase flow. The prototype of new schema-driven Siri AI is not included. Your local data may be included in device backups managed by your iOS settings. Copies you save elsewhere are under your control and are not removed by app erasure.

TestFlight itself automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider, independently of the optional local diagnostics toggle. Apple does not offer an opt-out for that collection while testing through TestFlight. [Apple's TestFlight privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

You can stop participating or decline any report. Ask the organizer to remove reports/contact details you previously shared through the agreed private feedback route.
