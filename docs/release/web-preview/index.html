Help with your daily rhythm

A little help.
Keep your progress.

Back up your routines, recover a saved copy or tell us what went wrong. The guidance here covers Daily Rhythm beta 0.1.0 (2).

Before updating, keep a backup

  1. Open Habits → Data & Privacy.
  2. Choose Export JSON and save the file somewhere you control, such as Files.
  3. Keep Daily Rhythm installed and update it in place. Check your routines and history afterward.

JSON saves your plans, archived plans and recorded history. Export history CSV is for reading history in a spreadsheet; a CSV file cannot restore your data.

Restore a saved JSON file

In Habits → Data & Privacy, choose Choose JSON backup. Select your saved file, review the plan and record counts, then choose Restore backup.

Restore works only when the app has no saved plans or history. It does not merge or overwrite existing records. Reminder preferences and iOS permissions are not included; select restored habits again in configured Controls and Shortcuts.

If your records are missing or restore is blocked, keep the app and backup file intact and contact support. Do not erase data or reinstall the app to troubleshoot.

Report a problem

Find the app version and build under Habits → Help & Beta. In your message, include:

  • Your app version/build and iOS version.
  • What you tried, what you expected and what happened.
  • Whether you used the app, a widget, a Shortcut or Siri.

Remove personal habit names or goals from screenshots. A routine backup is not needed for an ordinary bug report. If you use TestFlight, you can also open Daily Rhythm in TestFlight and choose Send Beta Feedback.

TestFlight automatically collects crash and usage information separately from the app’s optional diagnostics. Read about TestFlight and support data.

Optional pilot diagnostics

Diagnostics start off. In Data & Privacy, Observe a 30-day pilot enables a local observation period. You can use Export diagnostics JSON to review an aggregate report and choose whether to share it with the pilot organizer.

This is separate from Export JSON, which contains your actual routines. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and declining diagnostics does not limit tracking. Turning observation off deletes its local records.

Read what diagnostics contain.

Reminders and this beta

Reminders are optional. If an enabled reminder does not arrive, check Daily Rhythm’s reminder settings and its notification permission in iOS Settings. iOS controls delivery; manual tracking still works without permission.

This beta works offline without an account. Cloud sync, AI routine generation, routine-session Live Activities and purchases are not included. Ordinary App Shortcuts are available; Siri behavior depends on your device and settings.
